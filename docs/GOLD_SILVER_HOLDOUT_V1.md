# Gold, silver and holdout policy v1

Effective policy: `gold-silver-holdout/1.0`, 2026-09-13. The latest user instruction takes precedence over earlier design text: **M1 may be used for development while retaining its exposure history; M2, its descendants and future holdout lineages remain locked against tuning and training.** Historical receipts retain their original wording and outcomes. This document does not rewrite old evidence, authorize new native repairs or select an actual holdout.

## Quality is independent of use

| Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `ungraded` | Proposal or received evidence; necessary checks remain incomplete. |
| `silver` | Useful, traceable development material with explicit limits; automation alone may reach this tier. |
| `gold` | An authenticated, independently reviewed correction revision with the complete v1 evidence packet and actual measured human work. |
| `quarantined` | Rights, lineage, identity or integrity prevents use until resolved. |

A `requested_tier` is a claim, not authority. Schema validation and `gold_eligible_by_record` cannot register a real gold item. The custodian verifies source/session provenance and actual human identities outside the validator, then records corpus admission separately. Do not invent human reviews, measured time or asset counts. Synthetic tests, machine-only repair and unreviewed AI drafts do not become gold through a metadata change.

Keep chemical correctness, native editability, visual acceptance and human review separate. A final human repair does not convert the first automated failure into a pass. Preserve first output, every repair, failures, rejection and final decisions. Gold status does not authorize training or redistribution.

## Development and evaluation boundary

Use `development`, `fixed_regression`, `evaluation_reserved` and `quarantined`. The two evaluation subtypes form one evaluation bucket. Track source family, sample ancestry, semantic relationships, asset hashes and exposure before splitting. A source drawing, crop, native-format copy, redraw, paraphrase, correction, atom-ID rename or equivalent chemical rendering stays in the same lineage. An unresolved duplicate is quarantined.

M1 is exposed development material under the current policy. Its prior tuning remains visible as `exposed` or `exposed_and_tuned`; it cannot be called unseen. A historical M1-only ancestor's old fixed assignment does not make M1 permanently frozen under this new instruction. Explicit current evaluation assignments still prohibit fitting. Development permission does not certify its quality or grant rights, and the present delivery does not run M1.

M2 and all descendants stay evaluation-only, including chemically perturbed relatives and gold corrections. Canonical `M2` and any named M2 ancestor must retain `exposed` or `exposed_and_tuned`; tagging the known benchmark and moving it to `evaluation_reserved` cannot make it unseen. They cannot supply training pairs, layout coordinates, prompt examples, rule selection, tuning targets, cached answers or golden-geometry generation inputs. This restriction applies even where their licenses permit training. Future holdout ancestry is equally excluded. A record that descends from both an allowed source and a locked source inherits the locked restriction.

`exposed`, `exposed_and_tuned`, `unseen` and `not_assessed` have distinct meanings. Same-semantics derivatives cannot erase known exposure. Missing history is not proof of unseen status. M2 remains exposed fixed regression while a later genuinely new chemical perturbation with a distinct input identity may be an unseen input in its evaluation lineage. The named exposed ancestor remains recorded; this exception does not apply to same-semantics redraws or renamed inputs. That supports only input novelty within a known family, not family-disjoint generalization.

## Freeze and access

An independent evaluator chooses or creates future holdout input only after the required base qualification and immutable code/rule/style/adapter/cache freeze. Keep evaluator-only answers, chemical annotations, corrected native geometry and feedback out of generation and tuning access. Log the selected input hash, freeze hash, evaluator, time and each disclosure. No holdout is selected by this document or by synthetic contract tests.

A post-disclosure repair consumes that input's unseen status. Keep the original result and require a new independently selected input for another unseen claim. Never move failed evaluation descendants into development to improve the score. Broad reaction-family labels alone do not establish duplicate identity, but shared chemical/correction provenance does require leakage review.

## Rights and operational limits

Record evidence-backed `store`, `annotate`, `train` and `redistribute` grants separately. Public accessibility, university access and manual correction grant no additional rights. Unknown permission is not approval. Use the user's authorized private destination for restricted native assets; public GitHub holds source and sanitized evidence only.

The single-record checker enforces declared ancestry exclusions, exposure contradictions and use flags. It does not authenticate the ancestry list, perform cross-corpus chemical/visual deduplication, enforce evaluator access controls or establish an actual freeze. A collection-level manifest and custody audit must supply those checks before future admission or evaluation. No collection, tuning, training or publication occurs in this design delivery.
