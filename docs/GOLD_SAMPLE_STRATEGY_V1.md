# Gold sample strategy v1

Status: a plan for 20-50 authenticated correction samples, with **30 as the working target**, 2026-09-13. This is a proposed allocation, not an inventory. This delivery collects zero samples, runs no corrections and trains no model. Existing failures, synthetic fixtures and the c92a918 ungraded pilot are not promoted to gold. Follow [the correction contract](GOLD_CORRECTION_CORPUS_V1.md) and [current split policy](GOLD_SILVER_HOLDOUT_V1.md).

## Stage the collection

1. **Pilot 5.** After separate collection authorization and reviewer availability, obtain five first-party examples spanning proton transfer, SN2, E2, carbonyl and aromatic substitution. Exercise the entire first-output, native-reopen, actual human correction, timing and independent review workflow. Stop expansion if correspondence, rights or logging is unreliable. Report actual accepted and rejected counts; five attempted pairs need not yield five gold pairs.
2. **Coverage 10.** Aim for at least one qualified sample in each fine family below. Resolve gaps in geometry representation, reviewer agreement and final-size viewing. Retain every unsuccessful candidate as silver/ungraded or quarantined with its reason. Do not weaken gold criteria to fill a quota.
3. **Working set 30.** Expand only after the pilot process is repeatable. Balance families and overlapping stress tags; report the true number of unique, authenticated gold lineages. Twenty may be adequate for an initial diagnostic study; extend toward fifty only to cover a demonstrated gap, with the same review requirements. This is not a statistical representativeness claim.

The broad user-requested families are all represented: proton transfer, SN1/SN2, E1/E2, carbonyl/acyl, aromatic substitution, rearrangement and pericyclic. Family labels describe intended coverage; the chemistry reviewer decides whether a proposed example and its electron-flow account are scientifically defensible. No example is selected merely because an existing Composer template can already draw it.

## Stress coverage

Use overlapping stress tags across the thirty lineages: provisionally at least sixteen multi-arrow examples, twenty with explicit charge/lone-pair placement, twelve multistep examples and six with snake layout. Spread the multistep/snake cases across several suitable families, aiming at four broad families where chemically appropriate. These are planning targets, not current achievement or mandatory changes to chemical mechanisms. Never insert fictitious steps, charges or electron-flow arrows just to satisfy the table.

Include asymmetry and nearby competing anchors, dense labels, opposite orientations, compact versus extended fragments and multiple plausible geometric arrangements. Review charge/LP placement, head-to-target meaning, curve bends, crossings, final-size legibility and interstep spacing. Preserve electronically and chemically distinct roles across states; an ID shuffle is a robustness control, not another independent gold sample. A native-format copy, crop or alternate raster size does not increase the sample count.

## Candidate provenance and correction workflow

Prefer new first-party, chemically reviewed examples whose storage and annotation rights are documented. An AI draft may be the preserved first proposal; a real human then corrects the native drawing while actual active time is logged. The corrector's final revision is independently reviewed for chemical, editability, visual and human-evidence scopes. Keep the original draft, both native formats, native renders, all readbacks, complete correspondence and typed field deltas.

Potential licensed external material requires a later explicit source/rights review. This plan authorizes no scraping, restricted coursework import or collection from publications. Public availability alone does not establish the right to annotate, train or redistribute. Native render evidence must originate from the authorized ChemDraw execution workflow, not a screenshot reconstruction claimed as native geometry.

M1 may contribute exposed development diagnostics under the new policy, but is not automatically a gold sample. M2 and all its descendants are excluded from this proposed development set and all tuning/training, even after correction. Future held-out evaluation material is separately controlled and does not fill the thirty-sample development quota. Rearrangement coverage must use independently selected development lineages whose provenance is not derived from locked M2 answers.

## Measure the corpus process

At each stage report attempted candidates, unique lineages, accepted gold, silver, ungraded and quarantined counts; family/stress coverage; rejection reasons; actual human active minutes and wall time; and missing evidence. Report first-attempt automated quality separately from final human-corrected quality. Do not infer mean correction time from missing entries or fill them with zero.

Use the quality oracle's fixed rules for evidence and review, then inspect disagreement cases independently. Neither the 30-item target nor good validator results demonstrate universal Composer quality, an economical-model benchmark or production training readiness. Future training requires explicit authorization, licensed development data and a separate protocol; a gold collection plan is not that authorization.
