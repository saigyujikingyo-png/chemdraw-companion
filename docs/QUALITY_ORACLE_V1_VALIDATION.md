# Quality Oracle v1: first delivery verification

Verified locally on 13 September 2026. This delivery implements the requested protocols, strict schemas, a bounded pixel-metric oracle and a typed correction-record validator. It establishes executable controls and the next native-supervision gates. It does not establish reliable native automatic acceptance or complete Composer optimization.

## Observed results

- 130 quality tests passed: 20 calibration, 56 pixel/control and 54 correction-record tests. No failures, errors or skips.
- 36 retained corpus tests passed. The two imported corpus checker/test files match the accepted c92a918 blobs exactly.
- All five new JSON schemas passed JSON Schema structural validation.
- All 22 synthetic controls matched their declared target-metric outcomes: 9 pass, 12 fail, 1 unmeasured. Every returned observation result satisfied the result schema.
- Independent correction review recreated five former false-eligibility paths against the current complete baseline. Zero-session time with a positive epsilon, approvals preceding reopen evidence, reused first/final render, duplicate primary semantic occurrence and exposed M2 labelled unseen were all rejected by the intended checks.
- The synthetic archive was reopened, its ZIP integrity checked and all 98 manifest members independently checked for size and SHA-256. Its 99th entry is the manifest itself.

The exact versions, durations, source/policy hashes, results and expected private package descriptor are in [the machine-readable receipt](QUALITY_ORACLE_V1_VALIDATION.json). The [reproducible controls](../examples/quality-v1/README.md) preserve the source IR hash and independently supplied reference per case.

## Limits that remain

The twenty-two cases are separate partial synthetic images. They do not demonstrate all eight families on one complete native diagram, native object-layer provenance, exhaustive collision-pair enumeration or absence of hidden overlap. The validator recomputes observable pixels; it leaves ambiguous, missing or unsupported extraction unmeasured. Native common-scaffold position extraction is not implemented. Pixel-bound synthetic marker positions cannot certify native chemical atom positions.

Thresholds are independent but provisional, not qualified by human gold corrections. Source/reference custody, independently observable native layers, complete native inventory, native readback-to-IR correspondence, automatic matching, authentic human identity/timing and full final-size visual review remain process/integration work. The record validator checks supplied deltas and evidence structure; it is not a production native diff extractor.

The earlier c92a918 runtime/probe P2 remains open. This new prototype binds full calibration style and never enlarges port tolerances, but no existing runtime call site was changed. The historical 51 runtime tests are retained c92 evidence, not rerun or substituted for a new native test. Overall ChemDraw remains unaccepted; M1 has no new native pass, M2 is unscored, no holdout was selected, gold remains zero and actual human correction time remains unknown.

## Current policy and scope

M1 is allowed exposed development with retained history. M2, all descendants and future holdout stay excluded from tuning/training. Old manifests and receipts retain their historical hashes and assignments; new correction records apply the current v1 policy. The working corpus target is 30 authentic mechanism corrections within a 20-50 range, staged 5, 10, then 30.

No native execution, corpus acquisition, training, MCP/installer/host expansion, Composer repair or new product surface was performed in this delivery.
