# Adversarial Negative Plan v1

This plan qualifies a new Quality Oracle prototype and the evidence needed for a future gold correction corpus. It is not ChemDraw native acceptance, a new renderer, a Composer repair run, or a statement that the c92a918 calibration vulnerability has been repaired in production. The existing runtime and probe call sites remain separate until an implementation change and independent acceptance explicitly connect them.

## Dataset and experiment boundary

Current instructions permit M1 as a development/exposed reference. Preserve its original native files and record all derivative exposure; never rename it or its descendants as unseen evidence. M2 and every M2-derived input, image, geometry, correction and answer remain frozen and unavailable for parameter fitting, prompt/ranker tuning, calibration or new repair attempts. M2 may only be examined as authorized immutable regression evidence. Do not select a holdout in this task.

Use independently authored development scenes for adversarial construction. Keep one immutable, chemically valid reference and one controlled visual mutation per test. Identical chemical IR bytes/SHA-256 are required, together with unchanged atom identity, element, isotope, explicit/implicit H, bond graph/order, charges, radical/LP inventory, specified stereochemistry and electron-flow semantic endpoints. An unchanged IR hash alone does not prove that a drawn or native document retained those semantics: verify the actual before/after object readback and mappings separately.

Synthetic curves, masks and correction records test software behavior only. They are not native ChemDraw pixels, human corrections or gold examples. A gold record needs its separately required human review and authentic native before/after evidence; negative-control metadata cannot supply either. Do not infer measured human time from automatic run duration, and never convert an unknown time to zero.

## Eight independent metric families

Each family needs a positive control with margin, an isolated negative, and an exact boundary test. Measure the other seven families as well. If the mutation also breaks another family, retain it as a compound negative and do not count it as the isolated test for either family. Port identities remain unchanged throughout.

| Metric | Measurement and positive control | Isolated visual negative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visible tip to target | Extract the visible head tip from its render-bound object mask. Measure it against the declared atom/glyph boundary or a separately qualified prospective-bond port. A control endpoint is not the visible tip. The head remains outside unrelated and target glyph ink. | Move the terminal geometry so the visible tip misses the same semantic target while retaining the tail and adequate clearance. Do not change the target ID to follow the misplaced tip. |
| Tail to source | Measure the actual tail against the declared LP slot, atom/radical port, or sigma/pi donor bond. Use state-scoped, artifact-bound identity. | Move the tail away from its unchanged donor; retain the visible tip and final tangent. A neighboring LP cannot substitute for the declared slot. |
| Lone-pair direction | Measure both electron dots and their owner association, radial direction relative to the owner's local bonds/glyph, and the declared directional constraint. Missing or ambiguous directional intent cannot yield a directional pass. | Rotate the electron pair around its atom into the wrong declared sector without changing its inventory/slot, introducing collisions, or moving the participating arrow tail. Use a non-donor pair when isolation requires it. |
| All-ink clearance | Check every non-exempt primitive pair, including bonds, atom labels, charges, LP dots, radicals, curly and reaction arrows, conditions and captions. Exceptions name the exact object pair and local contact region; a third object cannot borrow them. | Close one eligible pair's ink gap below its fixed minimum while leaving all ports, bends and other distances valid. A bounding-box distance cannot substitute for an ink measurement. |
| Bend severity | Apply the policy's predeclared curve-shape measure, such as normalized arc/chord ratio and turning severity. Retain the exact evaluated path and sampling/error bound. | Increase the interior excursion or add an inflection while keeping both endpoints and terminal tangents fixed and retaining empty space around the curve. Do not change thresholds after seeing the result. |
| Head/glyph overlap | Test the actual head region against the actual target and unrelated glyph masks, independently of endpoint distance. A close endpoint and an overlapping head are different results. | Widen or relocate the rendered head to overlap one glyph while keeping a valid tip position and declared target. A calibration/style change invalidates the old calibration; this negative must use an independently bound matching measurement rather than edit the calibration to obtain a pass. |
| Step spacing | Measure neighboring state envelopes and their reaction connector/condition regions in reading order, in physical or bond-normalized units. Include electronic marks in the envelopes. | Translate one whole state toward its neighbor, preserving its internal geometry and chemical occurrences, until the spacing limit is crossed without producing another collision. Keep the intended step association fixed. |
| Common-scaffold consistency | Compare the declared common scaffold across states after the policy's rigid alignment, using a frozen semantic atom correspondence. Report residual displacement and orientation consistency; reflections and unapproved scaling are not rigid alignment. | Distort or inconsistently orient an unchanged scaffold while retaining actual graph/stereo semantics, valid ports and adequate spacing. Do not select a more convenient atom subset after observing the distortion. |

The current generic mask adapter's unsupported bond-sink measurement cannot become a pass by labeling its curve a familiar atom-sink case. Add a qualified bond-port measurement or return unsupported. Likewise, an empty object mask, absent LP slot, unresolved object mapping, invisible head or unavailable measurement is never an automatic pass.

## Fixed policy, boundaries and uncertainty

Freeze the metric definitions, eligible contacts, thresholds, units, artifact list and calibration descriptor before evaluating each batch. Tip-target and tail-source thresholds are separate fixed policy values. A scene must not choose or expand its own tolerance, and a learned score must not override a mandatory metric failure.

For an upper-bound metric with observed value `d`, total conservative uncertainty `u` and maximum `T`, pass only if `d + u <= T`. Fail when the entire uncertainty interval is outside the allowed region; otherwise report uncertain. For a minimum-clearance metric, pass only if `d - u >= T`. Exact boundary policy must be explicit and tested; numerical rounding must not turn an unresolved interval into a pass. Missing measurements, NaN/Infinity, negative uncertainty, wrong units, ambiguous identities and uncalibrated transforms fail closed or remain unverified, never pass.

Use scale and rotation controls that preserve the declared metric: translate the whole scene; rotate the complete scene, masks, port frames and directional constraints consistently; and test physical rescaling only with style, calibration and thresholds expressed in the corresponding units. Reusing a calibration from another stroke, font, head type/width/center size or native build is a deliberate rejection test, not an invariance test. Pixel resampling is synthetic evidence and does not replace a new native render at the required physical scale.

## Calibration trust and retained P2 controls

The new [calibration schema](../contracts/quality/calibration.schema.json) and [loader](../contracts/quality/calibration.py) read one separately supplied local calibration asset. They bind exact asset bytes/SHA-256, native-build declaration and all style fields: `font_family`, `font_pt`, `bond_pt`, `stroke_pt`, `head_size`, `head_width`, `head_center_size`, `head_type`, `head`, `tail`, `curve_type`. The head-size/width/center fields retain raw backend attribute numbers; they are not point distances. Extent and uncertainty use points.

The loader preserves the input limits and derives their conservative sum. This v1 profile restricts that sum to at most one bond length. It is metadata for measurement interpretation, not an addition to either port tolerance. The oracle derives the actual visible tip from bound render pixels and applies uncertainty conservatively. A build string and matching digest establish the declared binding only; neither authenticates a real native execution nor establishes coverage of every curve family.

Retain controls for the original c92a918 counterexample and the new prototype: a target displaced by 30 pt; scene tolerance/allowance `1e6`; calibration extent `1e6`; NaN, infinity, overflow and negative uncertainty; missing calibration; changed bytes with a stale descriptor; wrong descriptor hash/size; native-build mismatch; every style field mismatch including 0.6 to 1.2 pt stroke; duplicate JSON keys; and uncertain/unmeasured calibration. Recomputing a file hash after a style mismatch must not bypass the independent expected-style check. The new prototype rejecting these controls does not change the historical c92a918 result.

## Receipt and stop conditions

For every case record the immutable reference and mutated visual asset hashes, common chemical IR hash, exact mutation and affected object identities, preserved semantic checks, policy/calibration hashes, eight metric values and uncertainties, expected versus actual verdict, and whether the evidence is synthetic or native. Retain the first output, all failed attempts, automatic-repair counts and exposure lineage. Record absence explicitly instead of inventing observations.

A family is qualified only within its demonstrated measurement scope after its positive, isolated-negative, boundary, missing-data and applicable transformation controls behave as specified. A mixed result does not collapse to a global pass. Human visual approval, native reopen/editability, chemical correctness, individual metric qualification and corpus gold status remain distinct evidence. Stop a declared bounded experiment at its budget; a failed oracle result does not authorize another native repair or M2 tuning.

## Executable first-delivery coverage

The first run delivers 22 separate partial synthetic controls: nine target-metric passes, twelve measured failures and one ambiguous enlarged apex left unmeasured. All eight metric families have a measured positive and negative in this bounded scope. Two additional negatives put shaft ink through a heat caption and bring a head too close to a Br-shaped caption; the latter carries no bromine chemical identity. All cases preserve the same public proton-transfer IR bytes. The independent tests also reject stale scaffold coordinates after marker pixels move; native scaffold positions remain unmeasured without a qualified extractor.

These are not complete native scenes and do not yet demonstrate the other seven families passing after each isolated mutation. That requirement above remains a future native qualification gate. No M1 or M2 image was tuned, and no synthetic fixture is a gold sample. See the [controls](../examples/quality-v1/README.md) and [verification receipt](QUALITY_ORACLE_V1_VALIDATION.md).
